🍜 Restaurant options

  • Jia Jia Tang Bao: Soup dumplings near People's Square; approx. ¥35–70 pp.
  • Ren He Guan (人和馆), Xuhui: Traditional Shanghai cuisine, crab-roe rice and red-braised pork, approx. ¥150–250 pp. Not near Lujiazui — better as an alternative meal if willing to change the route.
  • Try hong shao rou, xiaolongbao and shengjianbao during the trip.

🔀 Flexible choices

If tired after Disneyland, skip the Shanghai Tower observation deck and spend more time at the Bund. If visibility is poor, save money and explore the riverside at ground level.

Critical Logistics

🚄 30 Dec arrival

After landing at PVG at 07:00, allow immigration, luggage collection and transfer to Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station. Target a train departure around 10:30–11:30, subject to the actual schedule; do not book an overly tight connection. High-speed rail to Shangrao often takes roughly 2–3.5 hours, then around 45–75 minutes by road to the valley.

🏙️ 31 Dec return

Aim to reach Shanghai by late afternoon for hotel check-in and rest before NYE.

🎢 1 Jan Disneyland

Expect heavy holiday demand. If celebrating past midnight, choose between an early park arrival and a more relaxed start with potentially paid ride access.

✈️ 4 Jan flight

Collect luggage and depart central Shanghai approximately 16:00–16:30, targeting PVG by 17:30–18:00 for the 21:10 international flight. Allow for holiday traffic.

🧥 Weather

Winter is chilly and can feel damp/windy; pack warm layers and comfortable walking shoes.

🎆 New Year's Eve

Do not assume an official Bund fireworks show; check the final city crowd-control notice and any venue-specific countdown arrangements.
