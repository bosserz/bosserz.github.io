# Shanghai — 4-Day Itinerary

Each day stays in one part of the city, so most moves are walks of under 2 km. Distances are straight-line, measured from the coordinates in `places.csv`. The `day` column in that CSV is filled in to match this plan, so the map's day filter shows each day's stops.

| Day | Area | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nanjing East Rd → The Bund → North Bund | Classic Shanghai, river at night |
| 2 | Old City → Pudong | Yuyuan Garden, skyscrapers, crab |
| 3 | Former French Concession (west) | Cafés, bakeries, tree-lined streets |
| 4 | West Bund → Jing'an → Xintiandi | Architecture, bakeries, last night out |

> I haven't checked opening hours. Check each place in Amap the day before, especially the cafés, which often open at 10:00 and close by 18:00–20:00.

## Swaps if you're short on time or energy

- **Day 2 is the heaviest.** Drop Long Time Ago, or move it to Day 1, since it's in the same building as New Sukiyaki.
- **Day 4 morning is the most out of the way.** If you'd rather sleep in, skip Gate M and Zhongshuge and start at Ren He Guan. The famous Thames Town Zhongshuge is about an hour away and only worth a half-day if bookstores are a priority.
- **If it rains,** swap Day 3 (outdoor walking) with Day 4.

## Practical tips

- **Transport:** Metro plus DiDi (works in English inside Alipay). For short taxi rides, show the driver the large Chinese name from the map.
- **Payment:** Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with a foreign card before you go. Cash is rarely used.
- **Reservations:** Canton 8 for sure. Weekend dinners at Tao Tao Ju and Xie San Bao can also have long queues, so get a number through the restaurant's WeChat/Dianping queue or arrive early.
- **Crowds:** Do the viral spots (13 de Marzo, Butterful, Matcha Wang, Yuyuan Garden) at opening time or on weekdays.
